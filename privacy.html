AppSafe Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Revised: November 23, 2026

Data Protection & Zero-Telemetry Principles

What Information AppSafe Chooses Not to Collect

AppSafe is built on a zero-telemetry philosophy. We do not require account creation, do not request personal names or phone numbers, and do not track individual device serial numbers. Browsing and downloading from our directory is completely anonymous.

Minimal Local Storage & Session State Protocols

AppSafe utilizes minimal functional local session storage solely to record live search queries and cached verification states during active installation workflows. We do not sell, rent, or trade session information to any third-party marketing companies.

Network Infrastructure & Compliance Information

Automated Server Logs and DDoS Protection Metrics

Like standard web infrastructure, our servers collect automated non-identifying technical data including user-agent strings, approximate country location, and timestamp for DDoS protection, rate limiting, and global CDN bandwidth balancing.

Frequently Asked Questions About User Privacy Rights

Do you store cookies on my mobile device?
We only use transient session keys necessary for verifying offer completion in the locker, which automatically expire.
