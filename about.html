Our Mission & Security Standards

Core Principles: Transparent, Tested Mobile Software

AppSafe was founded to provide smartphone enthusiasts with a transparent, rigorously tested library of mobile modifications, tweaked utilities, and enhanced game packages. We believe users should enjoy expanded device capabilities without exposing their hardware to unsafe binaries or warranty-voiding root exploits.

Testing & Quality Verification Standards (4-Tier Pipeline)

Every single mod published on AppSafe undergoes a 4-tier automated and human review pipeline before being made available for public indexing:

  • Static Malware & Signature Analysis: Codebases are verified against 60+ antivirus engines to guarantee zero spyware, adware, or trojans.
  • Permission Scrutiny: We reject any application that requests unnecessary permissions like background location, SMS reading, or contact harvesting.
  • Hardware Sandbox Execution: Packages are deployed across physical iOS 16–18 and Android 11–15 devices to verify FPS stability, battery efficiency, and crash resistance.
  • Anti-Ban Integrity Verification: Online game modifications incorporate local server-side bypass layers designed to safeguard player progress.
